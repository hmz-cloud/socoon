Soundproofing GFB Triplex

Three-layer engineered triplex composed of two moisture-resistant gypsum plasterboards (GVLVU) laminated with a specialized viscoelastic vibroacoustic sealant layer.

Innovative Floating Floors

Sound-insulating leveling coating applied in floating floor constructions as an elastic layer beneath the screed, even across irregular monolithic or precast structural slabs.

  • Levels uneven subfloors while isolating
  • Eliminates impact footstep & dragging noise
  • Elastic under-screed formulation
